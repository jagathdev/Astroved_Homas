⸎ ABOUT ⸎

CHANDI HOMAM

❖

ANCIENT VEDIC RITUAL.
DIVINE PROTECTION. ETERNAL BLESSINGS.

Chandi Homam (Homa) is a sacred Vedic ritual dedicated to Goddess Chandi, an embodiment of divine feminine energy and supreme power. This powerful ritual is performed to invoke her blessings for protection, courage, prosperity, health and overall well-being.

Rooted in ancient scriptures, this homam removes negativity, clears obstacles and brings harmony, peace and success into your life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Chandi Homa

What is Chandi Homa?
Chandi Homa is a powerful and elaborate fire ritual performed to seek the blessings of Goddess Chandi (Durga). It is renowned for clearing obstacles, destroying negative energies, and bringing immense prosperity and protection.
Who can perform this Homa?
Anyone facing significant challenges, health issues, or those seeking powerful divine protection and success in life can perform this Homa. It is highly beneficial for individuals and families alike.
How long does the puja take?
A traditional Chandi Homa is a comprehensive ritual that typically takes 4 to 6 hours depending on the specific procedures and the number of priests involved in the chanting.
Will I receive updates during the puja?
Yes, you will receive timely updates via WhatsApp or email. If you opt for live streaming, you will be provided a secure link to watch the entire process unfold in real-time.
What items are included in the puja package?
Our complete package covers all authentic samagri (ingredients) required for the homam, priest dakshina, and the shipping of the divine prasadam to your address.
What are the benefits of this Homa?
It removes fear, evil eyes, and negative planetary effects. It ensures victory over enemies, court cases, and provides mental peace, health, and abundant prosperity.
On which days is this Homa performed?
While it can be performed on auspicious days customized to your birth star, Navaratri, Ashtami, Navami, and Chaturdashi tithis are considered exceptionally powerful for Chandi Homa.
Can I watch the puja live?
Yes! We provide high-quality live streaming for most of our packages. You can participate virtually with your family from the comfort of your home.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations can be made up to 24 hours before the scheduled puja time for a full refund. As preparations require authentic materials, late cancellations may incur a small fee.
How will I receive the prasadam and blessings?
After the successful completion of the Homa, the sacred prasadam (including bhasma, kumkum, and blessed threads) is securely packed and shipped to your registered address via express courier.
